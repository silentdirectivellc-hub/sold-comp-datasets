The melt number is a floor for *metal*, not a price. Where it sits relative to actual closes is the
whole question: for war nickels in 2026 the floor sits **inside** the sold ladder, above large lots
and below singles, which means a big lot sells for less than the silver in it.

Recompute before you use these: multiply the fine ozt column by today's spot.
